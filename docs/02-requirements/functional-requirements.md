## Trigger and completion contract

This table makes the lifecycle boundary testable. The existing catalog defines the fuller flow, related rule range, and acceptance criteria. “Complete” means the stated business postcondition is recorded; it does not mean every related external process has completed. A failure must leave an explicit recoverable state and must not create the success postcondition prematurely.

| ID | Trigger and precondition summary | Successful postcondition | Failure or exception postcondition |
|---|---|---|---|
| RF-001 | Adviser/customer starts a customer record with required identity fields and authority to act | One customer/party record is active or observed, with duplicate-check and verification evidence | Potential duplicate or incomplete identity remains draft/under review; no silent merge |
| RF-002 | Evidence is uploaded or reviewed against a document requirement | Versioned evidence has owner, type, source, dates, review result, and access history | Invalid/expired/unreadable evidence is flagged; any mandatory gap blocks the dependent state |
| RF-003 | Product owner submits a complete version for publication | Approved product version has effective dates and frozen eligibility/pricing/calendar terms | Missing or invalid parameter prevents publication; prior used versions remain available |
| RF-004 | Customer/adviser submits an application meeting required completeness and consent conditions | Dated application snapshot is accepted for assessment and linked to the selected product version | Missing data leaves an incomplete draft; duplicate is linked/held rather than submitted twice |
| RF-005 | Complete snapshot is assessed with authorized source access | Versioned indicators, source evidence, missing-data list, and explainable recommendation are recorded | Provider/policy error leaves pending/incomplete/error; it cannot produce an implicit approval |
| RF-006 | Analyst submits a recommendation to a decision authority | Authorized approval/condition/rejection/return includes authority, rationale, policy version, conditions, and validity | Conflict, exceeded authority, stale evidence, or exception leaves the case blocked/escalated |
| RF-007 | Approved case enters formalization | Customer acceptance evidence and immutable accepted-term snapshot exist | Term mismatch, missing signature/acceptance, or unmet condition suspends formalization |
| RF-008 | Authorized operator sends an approved disbursement instruction | Provider confirms settlement or an authorized reconciliation establishes it; one disbursement movement is effective | Timeout remains unknown; rejection remains not disbursed; neither permits blind retry |
| RF-009 | Confirmed loan terms/value date are available for schedule generation | Schedule version has dates, components, formula inputs, and principal reconciliation | Invalid terms/calculation error prevents activation or retains the prior version; no negative principal |
| RF-010 | Payment message/manual receipt is submitted | One confirmed receipt has an identified allocation whose component total reconciles to applied funds | Duplicate, currency mismatch, unknown settlement, or conflict remains unapplied/under review |
| RF-011 | Customer requests a dated prepayment or settlement quote and supplies an instruction to apply | Confirmed funds update principal or settle the balance; successor schedule/closure is recorded | Expired quote is recalculated; insufficient funds do not settle; excess remains separately controlled |
| RF-012 | Contractual variable-rate review date arrives and valid index observation is available | New rate version is effective on its contractual date and only future accrual/schedule is updated | Missing/invalid index triggers contractual fallback or escalation; no retroactive alteration |
| RF-013 | Analyst/operations records collateral or a change/release request | Evidence, value, owner, scope, status, and coverage link are current and traceable | Unperfected/expired/disputed collateral is pending and excluded from effective coverage |
| RF-014 | A due amount remains unpaid at the applicable cut-off | Delinquency and collection case reflect overdue amount, age, permitted calculation, and activity | Payment in transit/dispute remains visible; adverse action follows policy and legal review |
| RF-015 | Borrower or analyst proposes changed terms for an existing obligation | Approved and accepted successor terms take effect with prior obligation/schedule retained | Until effective, old obligation remains; simulation alone does not change balance or due dates |
| RF-016 | Finance requests an approved write-off/recovery event | Write-off treatment is recorded separately and linked recovery remains reportable | Missing approval/accounting treatment blocks posting; debt is not automatically forgiven |
| RF-017 | Confirmed event must be corrected or reconciled | Linked compensating movement and reconciliation outcome preserve original evidence | Unknown external state is investigated first; repeated or excessive reversal is rejected |
| RF-018 | Authorized auditor/manager requests a report or history | Output records scope, filters, cutoff, generation time, and source evidence needed to reproduce it | Unauthorized scope is denied and audited; report data does not mutate source records |
| RF-019 | Administrator changes access/delegation or user attempts a protected action | Effective permission decision is evaluated by action, scope, amount, state, and validity | Revoked/expired/out-of-band action is denied; pending action is revalidated before completion |
| RF-020 | A business event requires a provider request or external message is received | Versioned message, correlation, validation, and result are linked to one business operation | Invalid/repeated/conflicting messages enter explicit rejection or reconciliation state |
| RF-021 | Customer or representative submits an inquiry, complaint, or dispute | Case has owner, linked evidence, status, deadline basis, and recorded response | Unverified identity or wrong-party access is blocked; financial evidence is preserved during review |

## Requirement change control

When a term is changed, update its business rule, process, story, acceptance criterion, traceability row, and (where financial) calculation fixture. Changing only a display or catalog sentence is insufficient if it changes when funds become effective, how much is due, who may decide, or what evidence is retained. The scope label M/V1/F is planning status; it does not replace an explicit business rule or current legal applicability review.
