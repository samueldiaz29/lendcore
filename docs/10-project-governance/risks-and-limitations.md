# Risks and limitations

| Risk | Effect | Documented treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Rule changed or misapplied | Non-compliant charge/rate/decision | Version regulatory catalog by date/category and obtain legal review before release. |
| Illustrative credit parameters | Over-indebtedness or false precision | Version thresholds, source, approval, and explainability; none is universal here. |
| Ambiguous dates/rates/rounding | Disputed balance and cumulative error | Contractual snapshot and financial cases; DP-003 awaits approval. |
| Delayed external confirmation | Duplicate disbursement/payment or false balance | Unknown status, idempotency, and reconciliation. |
| Third-party data dependency | Incomplete/inaccurate assessment | Consent/source/date, controlled fallback, and correction. |
| Operational ledger differs from accounting | Financial discrepancies | Reconcile; ledger does not replace general ledger. |
| Unperfected/overvalued collateral | Coverage may not exist in practice | Separate perfection state, appraiser, and expiry. |
| Profiling an individual | Bias, improper data, opaque decision | Minimize data, preserve human review, traceability, and privacy assessment. |
| Broad scope | MVP becomes undeliverable | Separate MVP, V1, and future; gate on dependencies. |

Limitations: this is product/business documentation, not legal advice, official accounting calculation, approved credit policy, or SBS compliance certification. Sources can change; maintain periodic regulatory review.
