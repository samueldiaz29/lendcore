# Scope prioritization

Criteria: end-to-end value, dependencies, financial risk, and legal/operational complexity. Blockers before funding: identity/evidence, capacity/decision, contract, verifiable schedule, confirmed disbursement, idempotent payment, ledger/reversal, audit, and reconciliation.

| Epic | MVP | V1 | Future | Rationale/dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer/documents | Individual/entity file, consent, review | External validation/customer portal | Advanced integrated KYC | Prerequisite for origination and privacy. |
| Product/origination | Simple personal/business | Vehicle/mortgage/tranches | Structured products | Mortgage/vehicle require asset registers and valuation. |
| Risk/decision | Explainable capacity/rules, human review, configurable bands | Segmented stress/models | Governed ML | Responsible approval comes first. |
| Contract/disbursement | Snapshot, acceptance, simulated single disbursement | Signature/live bank integration | Complex execution | No operation without traceable conditions. |
| Calculation/loan | Fixed French/German, day-count, principal grace | Variable rates/additional calendars | Specialized models | Validate fixtures/rounding before scale. |
| Payments/prepayment | Simulated upload/API, partial/full, reversal | Live rails/automated reconciliation | Advanced payments | Financial integrity is essential in MVP. |
| Delinquency/collections | Detection and manual case | Strategies/channels | Contextual automation | Reliable payments are prerequisite. |
| Collateral | Personal/real collateral workflow | Integrated valuation/registry | Legal enforcement integration | Manual record alone does not prove perfection. |
| Refinancing/write-off | Document states and restricted manual flow | Formal process | Advanced prudential management | High impact on regulatory treatment. |
| Reporting/audit | Balance, installments, movements, decisions, export | Validated regulatory reporting | Advanced analytics | Traceability from first movement. |
