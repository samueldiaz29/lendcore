# Payment allocation

## Configurable proposal

Separate receipt, settlement, identification, application, and reconciliation. After funds are confirmed, allocate to enforceable due items according to the applicable contractual/legal hierarchy and what was disclosed to the customer. Product configuration cannot contravene mandatory law. Proposed operational baseline: overdue enforceable obligations → accrued compensatory interest → principal → expressly due non-financial charges. Legal review must confirm exact priority and treatment of default interest, fees, and expenses before production.

An insufficient payment is distributed under the applicable hierarchy; record amounts allocated by component and remaining balance. An exact payment closes the covered obligations. Excess requires a clear instruction for prepayment/installment advance or remains unapplied; do not absorb it as revenue.

Example: due interest 20 and principal 100; receipt 70; assuming interest-first priority, allocate 20 to interest and 50 to principal, leaving 50 principal due; installment remains partially unpaid. Monetary postings are rounded to cents; all amounts retain PEN/USD currency.

Reversals compensate each allocation by component and regenerate derived balances from the effective date; do not re-execute the original payment.
