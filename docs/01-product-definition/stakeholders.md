# Stakeholders

| Party | Need / responsibility |
|---|---|
| Customer / representative | Apply, provide data, accept terms, pay, prepay, view/correct data, and file complaints. |
| Financial adviser | Identify needs, capture applications and documents; cannot alter assessment or approve their own origination. |
| Credit analyst | Verify evidence, analyze capacity/risk, and recommend a decision; cannot disburse. |
| Supervisor/approving authority | Decide within delegation; record rationale and conditions. |
| Operations staff | Control formalization, schedules, authorized maintenance, and reconciliation. |
| Disbursement staff | Execute an approved order and reconcile confirmation; separate from approver. |
| Collections staff | Manage permitted contact and arrangements; cannot change debt beyond authority. |
| Risk/Product/Legal/Finance | Govern policies, terms, exceptions, pricing, and regulation. |
| Auditor | Read access to evidence, logs, movements, and reports; cannot operate. |
| Platform administrator | Manage identities and configuration; cannot approve or alter balances through technical privilege. |
| Core, bureau, identity/valuation provider | External systems subject to contract, consent, availability, and reconciliation. |
