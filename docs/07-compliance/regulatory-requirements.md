# Verifiable regulatory requirements

## How to read this catalog

This is a research register for product analysis, not a compliance opinion. A cited law or resolution may bind a real supervised entity, but applicability can depend on license/category, product, customer status, channel, effective date, amendments, and transitional clauses. `LendCore requirement` translates a topic into a capability or control; implementing that capability does not prove compliance. Verify the consolidated official text and have Legal/Compliance map each obligation before a production use. Research cut-off: **9 November 2026**.

## Requirements for legal traceability

For every regulatory implementation, retain the official instrument, affected article/annex, consolidated version or amendment chain, effective-from/to dates, affected customer/product/channel, interpretation owner, and approval record. A source URL alone is not a legal interpretation. Any rate cap, disclosure wording, retention period, complaint clock, security obligation, or collateral perfection rule must resolve to the applicable current text before a bank policy can be configured.
