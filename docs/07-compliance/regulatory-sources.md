# Official sources consulted

Sources consulted as of 9 Nov 2026; re-check links when approving each release.

1. **SBS, Resolution 3780-2011, Credit Risk Management Regulation**, PDF and history: [official SBS PDF](https://intranet2.sbs.gob.pe/dv_int_cn/774/v6.0/Adjuntos/3780-2011.R.pdf), [SBS regulatory history](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/app/pp/INT_CN/Paginas/Busqueda/VerHistorial.aspx?NormaId=774). History shows amendments and future effective dates; use consolidation effective on the relevant date.
2. **SBS Resolution 11356-2008**, Debtor Evaluation/Classification and Provisions: [SBS PDF](https://intranet2.sbs.gob.pe/dv_int_cn/1097/v6.0/Adjuntos/11356-2008.r.pdf); [SBS notice on Resolution 2368-2023](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/noticia/detallenoticia/idnoticia/2680?title=SBS+modifica+el+Reglamento+para+la+evaluaci%C3%B3n+y+clasificaci%C3%B3n+del+deudor).
3. **SBS Market Conduct Regulation 3274-2017**, [SBS consolidated PDF v11](https://intranet2.sbs.gob.pe/dv_int_cn/1731/v11.0/Adjuntos/3274-2017%20actualizado.pdf); [SBS regulatory history](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/app/pp/INT_CN/Paginas/Busqueda/VerHistorial.aspx?NormaId=1731); [SBS summary of Res. 3240-2023 changes](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/noticia/detallenoticia/idnoticia/3701). The SBS history lists amendments, including 2025 versions; confirm current consolidation at release.
4. **BCRP Circular 0008-2021 and semiannual rate caps**, [official Circular PDF](https://www.bcrp.gob.pe/docs/Transparencia/Normas-Legales/Circulares/2021/circular-0008-2021-bcrp.pdf), [BCRP May–October 2026 notice](https://www.bcrp.gob.pe/docs/Transparencia/Notas-Informativas/2026/nota-informativa-2026-04-07.pdf). The official notice states a subsequent update date of 1 Nov 2026; that newer notice was not verified as of this review, so no current cap is asserted here.
5. **Consumer Protection and Defense Code**, updated Law 29571 text, [El Peruano](https://diariooficial.elperuano.pe/Normas/obtenerDocumento?idNorma=17); updated text identifies Law 31601 as an addition to prepayment rights.
6. **Law 29733 and DS 016-2024-JUS**, [ANPD/Gob.pe](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/anpd/normas-legales/6554453-n-016-2024-jus), [effective date 31 Mar 2025](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/anpd/campa%C3%B1as/128319-nuevo-reglamento-de-proteccion-de-datos-personales).
7. **SBS Resolution 504-2021**, [current SBS history](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/app/pp/INT_CN/Paginas/Busqueda/BusquedaPortal.aspx?normaId=2046), and [SBS bulletin](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/boletin/detalleboletin/idbulletin/1147).
8. **SBS Valuers Registry**, [related rules](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/supervisados-y-registros/registros/otros-registros/peritos-valuadores/informacion-sobre-el-registro-de-peritos-valuadores).
9. **Law 29733**, [Gob.pe / Congress](https://www.gob.pe/institucion/congreso-de-la-republica/normas-legales/243470-29733).
10. **SBS prohibited commissions and expenses**, [SBS consumer information](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/usuarios/informacion-financiera/comisiones-prohibidas). Check the current annex and product applicability before configuring any charge.
11. **SBS Regulation on complaints and requests, Resolution 4036-2022**, [SBS bulletin describing the regulation](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/boletin/detalleboletin/idbulletin/1248). Use the current consolidated text for deadlines and channel rules; this summary does not set a response deadline.
12. **Law 26702, General Law of the Financial and Insurance Systems**, [El Peruano updated text](https://diariooficial.elperuano.pe/Normas/obtenerDocumento?idNorma=100009), marked updated 13 Sep 2024; verify later changes before citing an article as current.
13. **SBS Resolution 02235-2026**, modifying related-party financing approval/conflict rules in the credit-risk regulation, [official El Peruano publication](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2552759-1) and [SBS notice](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/noticia/detallenoticia/idnoticia/3997). Published 11 Sep 2026; effective the following day.
14. **Law 31143**, [official El Peruano publication](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/1936151-1); **SBS Resolution 02192-2022**, [official El Peruano publication](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2086019-1), application provisions on maximum rates.
15. **Consumer Code Law 29571**, [El Peruano updated text](https://diariooficial.elperuano.pe/Normas/obtenerDocumento?idNorma=17), article 86 on prepayment; **Law 31601** must be interpreted alongside the current consolidated text, not as a substitute for checking it.
16. **Law 28587**, [El Peruano updated text](https://diariooficial.elperuano.pe/Normas/obtenerDocumento?idNorma=18), financial consumer protection law; read alongside its amendments and the SBS conduct regulation.
17. **SBS Resolution 01741-2026**, [official El Peruano publication](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/api/visor_html/2530621-1) and [SBS notice](https://www.sbs.gob.pe/noticia/detallenoticia/idnoticia/3947). Issued 1 Jul and published 2 Jul 2026; article 3 specifies that most amendments take effect 360 days after publication, with specified exceptions for article 3.2(v) and article 41-B effective the following day.
18. **SBS Resolution 04320-2025**, amending complaints/request channels and commissions/gastos rules, [official El Peruano publication](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2465965-1), published 6 Dec 2025.

This review did not locate one consolidated official text covering all regulations and amended annexes. Further regulatory diligence must verify concordances, articles, amendments, and transitional provisions for each product.
