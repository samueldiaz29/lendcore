# Regulatory framework and interpretation limits

**Research cut-off:** November 9, 2026. LendCore is a portfolio simulation, not a supervised institution or legal opinion. Applicability depends on license, SBS category, product, channel, consumer status, date, and amended rules. Legal and Compliance review is required before real-world use.

## Subject map

- **Credit risk:** Law 26702 and Credit Risk Management Regulation (SBS Resolution 3780-2011 and amendments, including Resolution 02235-2026 effective 12 Sep 2026); Debtor Evaluation and Classification and Provision Requirements Regulation (SBS Resolution 11356-2008 and amendments). Repayment capacity is primary and collateral is subsidiary (Law 26702, art. 222). Conflict-of-interest and related-party approval requirements depend on entity type and governance regime.
- **Conduct/transparency:** SBS Resolution 3274-2017 and amendments, including Resolutions 3240-2023 and 01741-2026; commissions/expenses under SBS Resolution 3748-2021 as amended. Res. 01741-2026 was published 2 Jul 2026: its specified customer-service principle and debt-regularization certificate provisions took effect the following day, while most changes take effect 360 days after publication. Resolution 8181-2012 is repealed and is not cited as current framework.
- **Rates:** Law 31143 amends Law 26702/consumer rules; BCRP Circular 0008-2021 and semiannual publications set caps for defined categories. Do not generalize a cap to every bank transaction.
- **Prepayment:** Consumer Code, Law 29571 art. 86 (read with amendments including Law 31601), recognizes total/partial prepayment in installment credit with reduction of compensatory interest through payment date and no penalty/equivalent charge; SBS regulation supplements channels/information.
- **Personal data:** Law 29733 and DS 016-2024-JUS, effective March 31, 2025; processing of risk data, profiling, rights, security, incidents, and transfers.
- **Security:** SBS Resolution 504-2021 as amended, with applicable regime depending on institution/channel.
- **Collateral/valuation:** Civil Code, Law 26702, and specific rules by collateral type; appraisers in REPEV are subject to SBS Resolution 3225-2021 and applicable prudential valuation rules.
- **Credit information:** Law 26702, Law 27489 on private credit bureaus, SBS reporting and bank secrecy; define purpose, accuracy, access, and correction.
- **Audit/retention:** SBS audit, governance/operational-risk, prudential reporting, and record-retention rules; exact term depends on record and concurrent duties. LendCore does not establish one universal retention period.

## Distinguishing obligations from design

Rules marked RN are proposed product controls; only explicit [C-XX] references are obligations researched here. Availability targets, additional controls, and maker-checker are good practice/design unless a specific rule makes them mandatory. These controls do not establish full regulatory compliance.
