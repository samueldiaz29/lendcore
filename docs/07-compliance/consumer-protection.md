# Financial consumer protection

Product requirements: before contracting, disclose amount, rate/type, currency, frequency/basis, schedule, cost/charges/insurance, default consequences, payment methods, and prepayment; deliver contract and supporting documents; ensure advertising is consistent; record acceptance and changes; enable payment/prepayment through applicable channels without prohibited charges; respect prepayment and reduce unaccrued compensatory interest as applicable; process complaints within configured legal deadlines.

SBS Resolution 01741-2026, published 2 July 2026, includes amendments to the Market Conduct Regulation. As of the 9 November 2026 research cut-off, the provision requiring a personal-attention alternative for certain automated-service flows (article 3.2(v)) and article 41-B on a no-cost debt regularization certificate on request (within seven business days once the user is current) are in force from 3 July 2026. Most of the resolution's other amendments have a 360-day delayed effective date. The effective-date exception matters: do not apply every 01741 disclosure/channel amendment as though already in force, and do not omit tracking it for future releases. Verify the consolidated SBS text before operational use.

Do not confuse “installment advance” with early amortization of outstanding principal. The interface and documents must present the choice clearly and show its effect on principal, interest, and dates. The SBS conduct rule describes customer rights and channels for both prepayment and installment advance; verify its current text and avoid extra process requirements where the regulation disallows them. Allocation order and expenses require legal review by contract/product. LendCore records presentation/acceptance evidence; it does not by itself establish legal adequacy.
